# PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)

A common hormonal condition behind irregular periods. It is manageable, especially when diagnosed early.

PCOS is one of the most common hormonal conditions in women of reproductive age, including in India. Despite the name, you do not need to have cysts on your ovaries to have PCOS.

## Common signs

- Irregular, infrequent or missed periods (cycles often longer than 35 days)
- Extra hair on the face, chin, chest or stomach
- Acne that does not go away, or oily skin
- Thinning hair on the scalp
- Weight gain, especially around the waist, or difficulty losing weight
- Dark, velvety patches of skin on the neck or underarms
- Difficulty getting pregnant

You may have only a few of these. A doctor usually diagnoses PCOS when two of these three are present: irregular ovulation, signs of high androgen levels (on blood tests or on your body), and polycystic ovaries on an ultrasound. Other causes, such as thyroid problems, are ruled out first.

## Why it matters

PCOS raises the long-term risk of type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol and problems with the lining of the uterus. Regular check-ups help catch these early.

## What helps

- Regular physical activity and balanced meals. Even losing 5% of body weight can improve cycles.
- Treatment from your doctor, which depends on your goals: regulating periods, managing skin and hair symptoms, or getting pregnant.
- Checking blood sugar regularly.

## When to see a doctor

See a gynaecologist if your cycles are often longer than 35 days, you have gone 3 months without a period, or you notice several of the signs above.
