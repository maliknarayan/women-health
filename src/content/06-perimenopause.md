# Perimenopause and menopause

The years when periods wind down. What changes, and what you can do about it.

**Menopause** is when you have gone 12 months in a row without a period. In India it often happens in the mid-to-late 40s, a few years earlier than the average in Western countries. **Perimenopause** is the transition before it, and it can last several years.

## Common changes

- Periods becoming irregular, heavier or lighter, closer together or further apart
- Hot flushes and night sweats
- Trouble sleeping
- Mood changes, anxiety or poor concentration
- Vaginal dryness, or discomfort during sex
- Needing to pass urine more often

## Look after your bones and heart

Oestrogen protects your bones and heart, so the risk of osteoporosis and heart disease rises after menopause.

- Get enough calcium (milk, curd, paneer, ragi, sesame) and vitamin D (sunlight, plus supplements if your doctor advises them).
- Do weight-bearing exercise such as brisk walking, yoga or climbing stairs.
- Check your blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol regularly.

## When to see a doctor

- Symptoms that affect your sleep, work or relationships. Treatments, including hormone therapy, can help.
- **Any bleeding after menopause**, even spotting. Always get this checked.
- Periods stopping before age 40.

You can still get pregnant during perimenopause. Use contraception until your doctor confirms menopause.
