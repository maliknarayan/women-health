# Heavy periods and anaemia

Heavy bleeding drains your iron. Anaemia is very common among women in India and often goes unnoticed.

According to the National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5), more than half of women aged 15–49 in India are anaemic. Heavy or long periods are one of the main reasons.

## Is my period heavy?

- You need to change your pad or tampon every 1–2 hours
- You pass blood clots larger than a ₹5 coin
- Your period lasts longer than 7 days
- You need to use double protection, or get up at night to change

## Signs of anaemia

- Feeling tired or weak all the time
- Breathlessness when climbing stairs
- Pale skin, pale inner eyelids or pale nails
- Dizziness, headaches or a racing heartbeat
- Hair loss, or brittle nails

## What helps

- **Iron-rich foods:** green leafy vegetables (palak, methi), dals, rajma, chana, jaggery, dates, ragi, eggs, fish and meat.
- **Add vitamin C** (lemon, amla, guava, oranges) to iron-rich meals to help your body absorb the iron.
- **Avoid tea or coffee** with meals, since they reduce iron absorption.
- **Iron and folic acid tablets** are available free at government health centres under the Anaemia Mukt Bharat programme. Take them on a doctor's advice.

## When to see a doctor

See a doctor if your periods are heavy or you have signs of anaemia. A simple haemoglobin blood test confirms it. Heavy bleeding can also come from fibroids, thyroid problems or other conditions that need treatment.
