# PMS and PMDD

Mood and body changes before your period, and when they become something more serious.

## PMS (premenstrual syndrome)

Most people who menstruate notice some changes in the week or two before their period. These include bloating, breast tenderness, food cravings, tiredness, irritability and feeling low. Symptoms ease within a few days of your period starting.

What helps: regular exercise, enough sleep, eating regularly, and cutting down on salt, caffeine and alcohol before your period. Painkillers such as ibuprofen can ease cramps and aches.

## PMDD (premenstrual dysphoric disorder)

PMDD is a severe form of PMS that affects a smaller group of people. In the days before your period it can cause:

- Intense sadness, hopelessness or anxiety
- Severe irritability or anger
- Feeling out of control or overwhelmed
- Problems at work, at home or in relationships

The pattern is what sets it apart: symptoms appear before your period and disappear soon after it starts, every cycle. Tracking your cycle for 2–3 months helps a doctor confirm it. PMDD is treatable with lifestyle changes, therapy and medicines.

## Get help now
