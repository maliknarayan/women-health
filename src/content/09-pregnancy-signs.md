# Early signs of pregnancy

What to look for, when to test, and what to do next.

## Common early signs

- A missed period (the most reliable early sign if your cycles are regular)
- Nausea, with or without vomiting, at any time of day
- Sore or swollen breasts
- Feeling very tired
- Needing to pass urine more often
- Light spotting around the time your period was due

Some of these overlap with PMS, so they are not proof on their own.

## When to take a test

Home pregnancy tests are sold at pharmacies and are accurate from the **first day of your missed period**. Testing with the first urine of the morning gives the most reliable result. If the result is negative but your period still has not come, test again after a week.

## If you are pregnant

- See a doctor early to confirm the pregnancy and start antenatal care.
- Start taking **folic acid** if you have not already. It helps prevent birth defects.
- Antenatal check-ups are free at government health centres, and you may be eligible for support under schemes such as PMMVY and JSY.
- Avoid smoking, alcohol, and any medicines your doctor has not approved.

## Get help urgently if

You have heavy bleeding, severe pain on one side of your lower abdomen, dizziness or fainting. These can be signs of an ectopic pregnancy or a miscarriage and need care immediately.
